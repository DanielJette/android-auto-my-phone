Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

Thank you for using Android Auto My Phone (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access, how it is used, and your rights with respect to your data.

1. Information the App Does NOT Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) to any external servers.

The App runs locally on your device and displays telemetry about your connected phone (battery level, signal strength, connectivity) only for your personal use when connected to Android Auto.

2. Local Device Data

The App may access device telemetry (such as battery level, signal status, connectivity state) from the Android APIs in order to display them within the App. This information is processed locally on your device and is not shared with the developer or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. If future versions include such services, this policy will be updated accordingly with links to the applicable third-party privacy policies.

4. Google Play and Android Permissions

The App may request permissions required by Android Auto and the underlying Android platform to access telemetry features. These permissions are only used to support the functionality of the App and are not used to collect or transmit personal data.

5. Children’s Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
